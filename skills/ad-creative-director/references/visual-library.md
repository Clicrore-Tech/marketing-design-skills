# Visual library (offline reference)

Use when live research is not possible. Patterns seen across local and performance advertising, with what to do about them.

| Pattern | Where you see it | Why it fails or works | Our response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photo grid of 20 or more faces | Results and testimonial ads | Proof, but no focal point | One hero face large, the rest as a number |
| Building plus logo plus phone | Local business banners | Everyone does it, nothing to remember | A specific moment inside with a real person |
| Red "SALE" band | Everywhere in festive seasons | Signals the season, not a reason | Lead with the reason, put the offer in the CTA |
| Huge number in gold | Results-driven brands | Works when the number is genuinely impressive | Keep it, add source and period, add one human face |
| Expert-led explainer covers | Strong creators | An expert face plus a promise beats logos | Use for Reels and YouTube |
| Local-language festive creatives | Regional brands | High local resonance | Use for greetings with a soft offer |
| Transparent price tables | Rare | Buyers want clarity, few brands give it | A clean price breakdown is a differentiator |
