# Intake

## The three-question rule

Ask one batch of at most three questions, only for what is missing and changes the result. If the brief is complete, restate it in one line and go.

Good questions:
- "Is this for a paid ad, an organic post or print?"
- "Who should stop for it: first-time buyers or existing customers?"
- "Do you have real photos of the product or place, or should I design around an illustration?"

Bad questions (decide these yourself):
- "Which font do you want?"
- "What colours do you like?" (use client memory and the brand)
- "Can you describe the design?"

## Brief record

Write this down before concepts and save the key parts to client memory:

```
CLIENT       <slug>
DELIVERABLE  <format and size>, <quantity>
JOB          <feel> -> <do>
AUDIENCE     <who, age band, what they care about>
MOMENT       <season, launch, sale, event>
COPY         <exact or to draft>
ASSETS       <logo, photos, brand rules, paths>
MUSTS        <non-negotiables>
DEADLINE     <date>
```

## Claims check (general advertising practice)

1. Objective claims (results, ratings, numbers) name their source and period.
2. Superlatives need third-party evidence on the creative.
3. Testimonials are real, current and consented.
4. Show typical results next to exceptional ones.
5. No guarantees you cannot honour.
6. Prices include all mandatory charges.

A working summary, not legal advice. Flag borderline claims and offer compliant wording.

## Formats

| Format | Size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed ad | 1080x1350 | Headline readable at 360px wide |
| Story or Reel ad | 1080x1920 | Keep text out of the top 250px and bottom 340px |
| Square post | 1080x1080 | Carousels and messaging apps |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280x720 | Bottom-right corner hidden by the timestamp. Under 2 MB. |
| Reel cover | 1080x1920 | The grid crops to the centre 1080x1440 |
| Link ad | 1200x628 | Headline plus one image idea |
| Website hero | 1920x900 | Text over the left 40 percent, check the phone crop |
| Print banner | per print size | Headline readable from 10 metres, CMYK-safe colours |
