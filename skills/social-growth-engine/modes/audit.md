# Mode: Audit

The account's own numbers outrank every rule in this skill.

## Ask for

Insights for the last 20 to 30 posts (screenshots are fine): reach, plays, average watch time, saves, shares, follows, profile visits and non-follower reach share. For ads: spend, leads or purchases, cost per result and how many leads were qualified.

## Compute and show the working

| Metric | How | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outlier multiple | reach / median reach of the last 30 posts | Real hit or normal day |
| Non-follower share | non-follower reach / reach | Did it travel? |
| Save and share rate | (saves + shares) / reach | Worth keeping or forwarding? |
| Follow rate | follows / profile visits | Does the profile convert? |
| Qualified lead rate | qualified leads / all leads | Are the ads reaching buyers? |

## Output

1. Top 3 posts by outlier multiple and the pattern they share (format, hook formula, who is on camera, topic)
2. Bottom 3 and the likely reason
3. Three things to do more of and one thing to stop
4. Next week's plan adjusted (hand to `modes/plan.md`)

Never invent numbers. If data is missing, say what you could not compute.
