# Mode: Leads and DMs

The money is in the conversation. Most businesses lose leads here by replying late or replying with a wall of text.

## The first reply (within 10 minutes during business hours)

```
1  thank them by name
2  answer exactly what they asked, in two lines
3  ask one qualifying question
4  offer the next step: a call, a WhatsApp, a visit, a booking link
```

Match their language and their length.

## Qualifying questions (pick the one that matters for this business)

- What are you looking for exactly?
- When do you need it?
- Which area are you in? (for delivery, visits or service radius)
- Is this for you or for someone else?

## Follow-ups (two, never more)

- **Day 2:** something useful, not a reminder. A price list, a guide, a short video answer.
- **Day 6:** a specific invitation. "We have a slot on Saturday at 11. Shall I hold it for you?"

Then stop. Log the lead as cold.

## Lead form follow-up (Meta lead ads)

Call within 30 minutes. If unanswered, send a WhatsApp in these words, filled in:

```
Hi <name>, this is <staff name> from <brand>. You asked about <product> on Instagram.
I can share the details here. <one qualifying question>
```

## Never

- Cold DM strangers in bulk
- Send a voice note as the first message
- Ask for personal documents or payment before a conversation has happened
