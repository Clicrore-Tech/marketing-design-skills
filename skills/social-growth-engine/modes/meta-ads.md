# Mode: Meta ads

Writes the copy and the structure. The user or their agency runs Ads Manager.

## Campaign skeleton (lead generation or sales)

```
Campaign   Leads or Sales objective, Advantage+ placements unless the creative is format-specific
Ad set 1   Core buyers: age and location that match the brand profile
Ad set 2   Broad: let the algorithm find buyers, creative does the targeting
Ad set 3   Retargeting: Instagram engagers and website visitors, 30 to 60 days
Ads        3 creatives per ad set: one Reel, one carousel, one static
```

## Copy for each ad

- **Primary text:** 3 versions. Short (one line plus ask), medium (3 lines), long (story with proof).
- **Headline:** 5 versions under 40 characters. Product plus outcome or deadline.
- **Description:** 2 versions.
- **CTA button:** Book now, Learn more, Sign up, Shop now or Send WhatsApp message.

## Lead form

- Intro: one line on what they get
- Questions: name, phone (prefilled), one qualifier, location
- Higher intent form type when lead quality is poor
- Thank-you screen: WhatsApp button and a promise of a call within 30 minutes

## Compliance check before delivery

Check every line against the claims rules in `references/season-calendar.md` and Meta's personal attributes rule. List any changes and why.

## Testing rhythm

Change one thing at a time: creative first, then audience, then form. Judge on cost per qualified lead or cost per purchase, not cost per click. Ask the business to mark lead quality weekly.
