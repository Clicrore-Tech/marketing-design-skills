# Mode: Comments and replies

## Under the brand's own posts

Sort every comment before writing anything:

| Bucket | Example | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KEYWORD | "PRICE" | Reply "Sent to your DM" and send the promised thing |
| LEAD | "Do you deliver to Whitefield?" | Real answer in public, then "DM us to book" |
| QUESTION | "Is it vegan?" | Answer briefly, then turn it into the next Reel |
| PRAISE | "Loved it!" | Thank by name, 3 to 8 words |
| COMPLAINT | "Too expensive" | Calm and factual, offer a call. Never argue in public. |
| NOISE | spam, bots | Hide or ignore |

Reply in that order. Replies in the first hour after posting matter most.

Complaints about safety, health or legal issues go to the owner immediately. Do not draft a public reply for those, flag them.

## On other accounts (engagement rounds)

Comment on local pages, partner brands, creators and customers. Comments must add something: a fact, a tip, a real question. Never "Great post" or emoji strings. Never pitch in someone else's comments.
