# Mode: Carousel

Carousels get saved and forwarded. They are the best format for anything people want to keep: steps, comparisons, checklists, price breakdowns.

## When to use it

Use a carousel for sequence or comparison. If the idea is one claim, make a Reel or a single infographic instead.

## Structure (6 to 9 slides, 1080x1350)

```
1      COVER    the hook, six words or fewer, readable as a thumbnail
2      STAKE    why it matters, one sentence. Also works as a second cover.
3-N    ONE IDEA PER SLIDE  headline 3 to 7 words, under 25 words below it
N+1    PROOF    a number, a result or a photo that backs the idea
LAST   ASK      one action plus the contact
```

## Deliver

- Slide-by-slide copy in a table: slide, headline, body, visual direction
- Visual direction a designer or the `infographic-studio` skill can render
- The caption
- One line for the Story that shares this carousel

## Carousel ideas that work for most businesses

- "Which one is right for you": a decision flow from needs to product
- "The price, fully broken down": every component, no surprises
- "What our best customers do differently"
- "How it works in 4 steps"
- "Myths people believe about <category>"
