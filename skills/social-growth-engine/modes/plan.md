# Mode: Weekly plan

Run once a week. Output a table the person posting can follow without asking anything.

## Inputs

- The brand profile and the current phase in `references/season-calendar.md`
- What happened this week (orders, launches, visitors, wins, mistakes)
- Last week's top post from `modes/audit.md` if available

## The week (4 to 5 feed posts, Stories daily)

| Slot | Format | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Reel | Expert tip: one useful thing the audience can use today |
| Tue | Carousel | A guide that gets saved: steps, comparisons, checklists |
| Wed | Reel | Proof: the process, the product, the place |
| Thu | Single infographic or carousel | Numbers, results, reviews (with sources) |
| Fri or Sat | Reel | Customer voice or team story |
| Daily | Stories | Behind the scenes plus one sticker ask |

During peak season add one fresh ad creative per week.

## Deliver

A table: day, format, hook, who is on camera, what to shoot, caption ask, posting time. Start with 7 to 9 am, 1 to 2 pm and 7 to 10 pm local time, then adjust using the account's own insights.

Add a list of 10 accounts to engage with this week.
