# Mode: Story

Stories are for people who already follow. Their job is trust and conversations, not reach.

## Daily shape (3 to 6 frames)

```
1  OPEN    something happening today, with a face or hands in it
2  MIDDLE  the detail: the process, the product, the result
3  ASK     one sticker (see below)
4  CLOSE   the answer, the outcome or tomorrow's teaser
```

## Stickers and their jobs

| Sticker | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| Poll | Cheap engagement. "This colour or that one?" |
| Quiz | Teaching moments that show expertise |
| Question box | Collecting real customer questions. Answer the best ones as Reels. |
| Link | Booking page, catalogue, lead form |
| Countdown | Launches, sale deadlines, events |

## The DM funnel

Frame 3 asks a question whose answer is a reason to talk: "Reply PRICE and we will send the full price list." Every reply is a warm lead for `modes/leads-and-dms.md`.
