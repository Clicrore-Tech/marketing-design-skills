# Mode: Profile

The profile is a decision screen. Someone arrives from one Reel and decides in seconds: is this what I want, is it near me, is it legit, how do I contact them.

## Score out of 100

| Item | Points | Full marks looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name field | 10 | Brand name plus a searched phrase: "Crust Bakery Indiranagar" |
| Bio line 1 | 15 | What it is plus the proof: "Sourdough baked at 4 am. 4.9 on Google, 2,100 reviews." |
| Bio line 2 | 10 | Location, hours or delivery area |
| Contact | 15 | WhatsApp or call button live, number in bio |
| Link | 10 | Goes to a booking, order or lead page, not the home page |
| Highlights | 10 | Menu or services, Prices, Reviews, How to order, Location (named plainly) |
| Pinned posts | 10 | Best proof post, best Reel, how-to-buy carousel |
| Grid consistency | 10 | Recognisable covers, readable at thumbnail size |
| Recent activity | 5 | Posted in the last 3 days |
| Social proof | 5 | Reviews highlight with real customers |

Show the scored table, the total, then rewrite the parts that lost the most points, in that order. Be honest. Most business profiles score 30 to 50 on the first pass.
