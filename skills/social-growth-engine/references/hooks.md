# Hook formulas for Reels

Each formula: the template, an example and the way it usually goes wrong.

| # | Formula | Template | Example | Ruined when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The number | "<number> <thing> in <time>" | "38,000 classes. One habit our regulars share." | The number is not surprising for this audience |
| 2 | The customer question | "Customers ask us <question> every day" | "Customers ask us if a beginner can join. Here is the honest answer." | The answer is vague |
| 3 | The myth | "<common belief> is wrong" | "You do not need a gym membership to get strong." | It insults the viewer |
| 4 | The mistake | "The mistake <who> makes in <moment>" | "The mistake everyone makes on their first wedding cake order." | No concrete mistake follows |
| 5 | The before and after | "<before> to <after>" | "From a dead shop front to 140 walk-ins a day." | No consent or no proof |
| 6 | The inside look | "What <place> looks like at <time>" | "What our bakery looks like at 4 am." | The footage is not interesting |
| 7 | The comparison | "<A> vs <B>: which one for <who>" | "Laser vs peel for someone with sensitive skin." | It ends with "it depends" |
| 8 | The countdown | "<N> days left for <deadline>" | "3 days left to book Diwali orders. Do this today." | The date is fake |
| 9 | The expert's rule | "The one thing I tell every <customer type>" | "The one thing I tell every first-time home buyer." | The tip is generic |
| 10 | The real cost | "What <thing> really costs" | "What a 2 BHK interior really costs in 2027." | Hidden costs are left out |
| 11 | The local | "<City> people, <direct statement>" | "Pune runners, this is the route nobody uses." | It sounds like a hard sell |
| 12 | The POV | "POV: <relatable moment>" | "POV: your client wants it by tomorrow." | Acted badly |
| 13 | The step list | "<N> steps to <outcome>" | "4 steps to a website that gets calls." | Steps are padded |
| 14 | The confession | "We used to <thing>. Then <change>." | "We used to discount everything. Then sales went up when we stopped." | It is not true |
| 15 | The challenge | "Can you <task> in 10 seconds?" | "Can you spot the fake leather in 10 seconds?" | Too easy or too hard |

## What makes hooks fail

- Greetings: "Hi everyone, welcome back". Nobody came to be greeted.
- Brand name first: "XYZ presents". The viewer does not care yet.
- The offer as the hook: "50% off". That is the ask, not the hook.
- Vague superlatives: "The best salon in town".
