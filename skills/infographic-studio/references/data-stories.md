# Data stories

What businesses actually have, what buyers actually read and the honest way to show it.

## The five questions every marketing graphic answers

A strong graphic answers one loudly and at most two quietly.

1. **Does it work?** Results, outcomes, before and after, wins.
2. **Do people like it?** Reviews, ratings, repeat customers, referrals.
3. **Can I trust them?** Years in business, certifications, team, guarantees they can honour.
4. **Can I afford it?** Prices, packages, offers, value against alternatives.
5. **How do I start?** Steps, dates, slots left, contact.

## Data types and how to show them

| Data | Typical source | Best hero form | Honest framing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer results | Case studies, records | Before and after pair, progress staircase | Name the period and the sample size. "Average of 212 clients", not one outlier. |
| Customer count | CRM, POS | Pictogram army (1 figure = N customers) | State N in the legend. |
| Ratings and reviews | Google, marketplace | Star wall with one real quote | Name the platform and review count. |
| Milestones and awards | Certificates | Honour board or medal row | Exact award name and year. |
| Growth | Sales, followers, orders | Staircase or rising road | Show the start point. Do not crop the axis to exaggerate. |
| Prices | Price list | Stacked coins or split bar vs market average | Include every mandatory charge. |
| Packages | Offer sheet | Three tiers side by side, one marked "most chosen" | Only mark it if it is true. |
| Process | Your workflow | Road, train route or calendar strip | Real dates and durations. |
| Capacity left | Booking system | Seat or slot map | Only when true and current. Fake scarcity kills trust. |
| Team | HR | People grouping with roles | Count only real staff. |

## Writing the headline

- Lead with the number when the number is the proof: "38,000 classes in 2026".
- Lead with the reader when the number needs context: "Your plan, built from 38,000 classes of data".
- Keep it under eight words.
- Never use "best", "No.1" or "top" without the source on the canvas.
